Responsible Gaming

Our picks are for entertainment. If betting or trading stops being fun, stop, and reach out. Help is free, confidential and open 24/7.

Need help now?

Call or text the National Problem Gambling Helpline at 1-800-MY-RESET (1-800-697-3738), or chat online. It is run by the National Council on Problem Gambling. Free, confidential, 24/7.

The honest truth

  • Over time, most people who bet on sports lose money. The sportsbook’s built-in cut makes sure of that.
  • Every pick can lose, including market favorites. Run It Bet is a filter, not an investment strategy: in our own real-money test the picks showed no reliable edge over the market. Read why.
  • Picks are entertainment and information, never a way to make an income.
  • Only use money you can afford to lose, and never chase losses.
  • 18+ only, and only where it is legal for you.

Warning signs

  • Betting more than you planned, or more often.
  • Chasing losses: betting again to win back what you lost.
  • Borrowing money, or using money meant for bills, to bet.
  • Hiding or lying about how much you bet.
  • Feeling restless, anxious or irritable when you are not betting.
  • Letting work, school or family slide because of betting.

If any of these sound familiar, please reach out to the helpline above.

Tools that help

  • Self-exclusion: most states with legal betting let you ban yourself from sportsbooks for a set time (often 1 year, 5 years, or life). Ask your state’s gaming commission, or ask the helpline to point you to it.
  • Limits: sportsbooks and Kalshi let you set deposit, time and loss limits in your account settings.
  • Support groups: Gamblers Anonymous, and Gam-Anon for family and friends.
  • Counseling: the helpline can connect you with a counselor near you, including in Texas.
