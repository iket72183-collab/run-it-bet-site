How does parlay math work?

A parlay joins two or more picks into one bet that pays only if every pick wins. Warning: parlays multiply your risk faster than your reward, and Run It Bet does not recommend them. The payouts multiply, so the prize grows fast — but so do the odds against you. Example: a $10 parlay on one pick at -110 and one at +150 returns about $47.73 if both win (about $37.73 profit), versus about $19.09 and $25.00 if you bet $10 on each alone. The catch: two picks that are each a coin flip only both win about 1 time in 4, and three only about 1 in 8. One loss sinks the whole ticket. Sportsbooks may also price a parlay differently from simple multiplication or refuse related picks. Our picks are meant to be taken one at a time. On Kalshi, each contract is a separate purchase: costs and payouts add up; they do not multiply.
