Instructions for your assistant

The site publishes at most two snapshots a day: at 8:30 AM Central, and at 9:30 AM Central (up to 9:35 if the lows finish late). If a snapshot is missed or publishing is paused, the feed stays as it was, so it may be old. Prices in the feed are snapshots from when each pick locked, never live, and there are no live scores. All times are ISO 8601 UTC; show them to me in Central time. A time that is null or missing means not recorded: never treat it as recent.

Each check:
1. Fetch https://www.runitbet.com/api/agent/today.json (JSON, no login). If it can't be fetched or isn't valid JSON, send me nothing and don't guess; if I ask, say the feed was unavailable.
2. If the feed has a "snapshot" object:
   - It must have an "id" and a valid "sitePublishedAt" time that is not in the future. If not, the feed contradicts itself: send me nothing, and tell me about it if I ask.
   - If its id matches the last snapshot you saw, or its sitePublishedAt is not later than that one's, there is no update: send me nothing.
   - Otherwise remember its id and sitePublishedAt as the last snapshot you saw, and continue.

3. "day" is the Central-time date the picks are for. If it isn't today's date in Central time, the picks are old: send me nothing. If I ask, tell me the date they're for and that they're old.
